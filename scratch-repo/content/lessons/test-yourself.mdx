## Estimation drill

A chat app sends **100 billion messages a day**, 100 B each, reads:writes = 1:1, replication factor 3. Find the write QPS and its peak, total QPS, 30-day storage, ingress and egress, and the system shape. How does its QPS compare with Google Search?

Work it on paper first, then use the estimator to check (it opens on the chat preset).

<Interactive name="estimator" variant="chat" />

<DeepDive title="Show the drill answers">

- **Writes:** 10¹¹ ÷ 10⁵ = 1M QPS (peak \~3M).
- **Total with reads:** 2M QPS (peak \~6M).
- **Storage:** 10 TB/day × 30 × 3 ≈ 900 TB.
- **Ingress = egress** ≈ 100 MB/s.
- **Shape:** a write-heavy stream (see [Three system shapes](/learn/system-shapes)).
- **Compared with Google Search:** about 20× its \~100k QPS.

</DeepDive>
